ABOUT THE PLATFORM

AI Data Scientist is an intelligent data analysis platform designed to help people explore, understand and work with data without requiring advanced technical knowledge.

OUR MISSION

Data should answer questions.

Working with data often involves multiple tools, complicated workflows and a strong understanding of statistics and programming.

AI Data Scientist aims to simplify that process by bringing data profiling, analysis, visualization and natural-language interaction into one platform.

Better decisions.
